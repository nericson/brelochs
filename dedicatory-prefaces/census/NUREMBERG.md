# The four German forecasts in Nuremberg

Germanisches Nationalmuseum, Bibliothek, **Postinc 8º Nw 2871**. Jonathan Green lists four Brelochs *practicas* under this shelfmark. The bracketed year identifies the annual item. Green gives the language as German and marks all four with an asterisk, his sign for an edition consulted in the original or in facsimile. A shared shelfmark establishes a common location. It does not tell us whom any particular preface addresses. [Green, *Printing and Prophecy* (2012), appendix, pp. 156 and 162](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/gdc/gdcebookspublic/20/20/70/73/92/2020707392/2020707392.pdf)

## Public image search, 20 November 2026

The museum’s online catalogue author search did not bring up these four items. In the museum’s separate digital library, the *Postinkunabeln* collection covering 1501–1550 displayed 59 titles. Checking the complete list for Brelochs and for the shelfmark, including 2871 alone, yielded no entry for this volume. The list includes other Nw shelfmarks and a practica by Johannes Lichtenberger. Searches pairing the author, variant forms, forecast years and shelfmark with union-catalogue or digitization results yielded no page images of these four German openings. The observation concerns the public interfaces checked on this date. Photography already held off-line remains unknown. [GNM digital library, Postinkunabeln](https://dlib.gnm.de/) · [GNM library catalogue](https://webopac.gnm.de/) · [Union-catalogue search record](README.md#search-record)

Several easily confused hits concern different objects. Green’s Nw 2869 [1533] belongs to Jeremias Brotbeih(e)l. The Geneva *Prognostication dAllemaigne* printed by Wygand Koeln in 1533 belongs to Johann Carion. Brelochs’s French forecast under consideration was printed by Gabriel Pomar. Herbst also points tentatively to Nw 2871 for an already documented forecast for 1528 and cites a published title-page illustration. A title image for 1528 cannot recover the openings for the four later years. [Green, appendix, pp. 162–63](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/gdc/gdcebookspublic/20/20/70/73/92/2020707392/2020707392.pdf) · [Geneva, Carion and Pomar catalogues](https://www.e-rara.ch/gep_g/nav/index/title?max=100&query=p) · [Herbst, Brelochs bibliography](https://www.presseforschung.uni-bremen.de/dokuwiki/doku.php?id=brelochs_anton)

All four German witnesses have bibliographical status in the census. None supplies a text for the German polemic register. A comparison of the German and French for 1533 will require a title leaf, the opening through its transition to the forecast, and the imprint if present. These are still outstanding.
