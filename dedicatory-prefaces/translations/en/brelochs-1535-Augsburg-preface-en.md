Steadfast, honorable, prudent, and wise lords: I first offer Your Worships my most willing and diligent service. Gracious and dear lords in authority, in order to maintain my longstanding custom, I have again undertaken to describe the future events of this present year, fifteen hundred and thirty-five. Accordingly, I have drawn them from the teaching of those learned in the stars and have once again set them down in a practica, which I present to Your Worships in the customary manner. I humbly ask that you graciously accept it from me, interpret it in the most favorable light, and continue to regard me as commended to your care as your humble and willing servant. Dated on the Sunday after St. Pantaleon’s Day, the second of August, in the year 1535 [as printed].

## Translation notes

1. The dedication prints M. D: XXXV. (1535), as does the alternate Augsburg witness. This potentially problematic date is retained; no correction to 1534 is imposed.

2. Gebietenden identifies the civic authorities as those who govern or command; it is rendered with “lords in authority.”

3. The feast reference and numbered day are retained without attempting to reconcile them.

4. This alternate Augsburg witness has the same transcribed German dedication as the primary Munich witness. Its English wording is therefore identical; its source links identify the Augsburg copy separately.
