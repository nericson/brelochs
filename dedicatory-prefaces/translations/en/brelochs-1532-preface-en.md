To the honorable, prudent, respected, and wise mayors and council of the city of Schwäbisch Hall, my gracious and dear lords.

*[German passage, transcribed lines 1–3](../../tei/brelochs-1532-preface.xml#L56)*

<!-- English segment 2; source range line-1532-1-4 through line-1532-1-15, inclusive. -->
Honorable, prudent, respected, and wise lords: I first offer Your Worships my most willing and diligent service. Gracious and dear lords, I have once again undertaken, so far as my limited understanding permits, to disclose what the course of the heavens signifies for us in the year 1532, and have condensed it as briefly as possible into a small prognostication. I know no one to whom I could more fittingly dedicate this judgment than Your Worships. I therefore ask that you graciously accept from me these future events, thus briefly assembled, and interpret them with your customary kindness. May you also continue to regard me as commended to your care as your humble and willing servant. Dated on the Sunday after St. Bartholomew’s Day, the twenty-seventh of August, in the year 1531.

Dr. Antonius Brelochs.

## Translation notes

1. Judicium means an astrological judgment or forecast here.

2. The weekday, feast reference, and date are retained as printed; no calendar conversion is imposed.
