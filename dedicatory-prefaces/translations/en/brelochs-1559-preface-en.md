To the steadfast, honorable, prudent, respected, and wise lords, the mayors and council of the praiseworthy city of Schwäbisch Hall in the Holy Roman Empire, my gracious lords in authority.

*[German passage, transcribed lines 1–6](../../tei/brelochs-1559-preface.xml#L60)*

<!-- English segment 2; source range line-1559-1-7 through line-1559-2-7, inclusive. -->
Steadfast, honorable, prudent, and wise lords: I first offer Your Worships my most willing and diligent service. Gracious and dear lords, so as not to depart from my longstanding practice and custom, I have once again undertaken, according to my limited understanding, to set down and write something concerning the courses, revolutions, influences, and positions of the planets, together with their natural inclinations, for the coming year 1559. I have thus composed, assembled, condensed, and arranged this as briefly as possible in a prognostication, which I deliver and submit to Your Worships in the customary manner. I humbly ask that Your Worships graciously accept it from me, interpret it in the most favorable light, and graciously continue to regard me as commended to your care as your humble and willing servant.

Given at Schwäbisch Hall on St. George’s Day, the twenty-third day of April, in the year 1558.

So that this practica of mine may be understood more clearly, I have divided it into eight chapters.

*[German passage, transcribed lines 7–30](../../tei/brelochs-1559-preface.xml#L67)*

<!-- English segment 3; source range line-1559-2-8 through line-1559-2-11, inclusive. -->
Your Worships’ obedient servant,
Dr. Antonius Brelochs.

*[German passage, transcribed lines 31–34](../../tei/brelochs-1559-preface.xml#L94)*

## Translation notes

1. The forecast year is 1559; the dedication is dated April 23, 1558.

2. The eight-chapter statement is included because the transcription includes it before the signature.

3. The signature’s spelling Brellochs is normalized to Brelochs in English; the source spelling is retained in the German TEI.
