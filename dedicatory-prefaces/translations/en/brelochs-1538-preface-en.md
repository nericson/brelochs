To the steadfast, honorable, prudent, respected, and wise mayors and council of the city of Schwäbisch Hall, my gracious and dear lords in authority.

*[German passage, transcribed lines 1–4](../../tei/brelochs-1538-preface.xml#L56)*

<!-- English segment 2; source range line-1538-1-5 through line-1538-1-17, inclusive. -->
Steadfast, honorable, prudent, and wise lords: I first offer Your Worships my most willing and diligent service. Gracious and dear lords in authority, I have once again undertaken, according to my limited understanding, to disclose what the influences of the stars and the disposition of the heavens signify and threaten for us in the year 1538. I have thus set this down and assembled it as briefly as possible in a prognostication, which I dedicate and present to Your Worships in the customary manner, with the earnest and humble request that you graciously accept it from me and, with your customary kindness, interpret it in the most favorable light. May you also continue to regard me as commended to your care as your humble and willing servant. Given on St. Margaret’s Day, the thirteenth day of July, in the year 1537.

Dr. Antonius Brelochs.

## Translation notes

1. Hewmonat is rendered July. Both the feast reference and its printed numbered date are retained.

2. The contracted signature Anthonꝰ is represented by the conventional Latin form Antonius; this is a translation convention, not a change to the German witness.
