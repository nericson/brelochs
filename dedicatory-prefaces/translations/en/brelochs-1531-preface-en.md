<!-- English segment 2; source range line-1531-1-4 through line-1531-1-14, inclusive. -->
Honorable, prudent, respected, and wise lords: I first offer Your Worships my most willing and diligent service. Gracious and dear lords, I have once again undertaken, according to my limited understanding, to disclose what the influence of the stars and the disposition of the heavens signify and threaten for us in the year 1531. I have also set this down as briefly as possible in a practica, which I present to Your Worships in the customary manner, with the earnest and humble request that you graciously accept it from me and continue to regard me as commended to your care as your humble, obedient, and willing servant. Calculated and written at Schwäbisch Hall on the twelfth day of June, in the year 1530.

## Translation notes

1. The opening Erern is awkward in the working transcription; the repeated honorific formula is translated conventionally.

2. Brachmonat is rendered June. The year forecast is 1531; the dedication is dated 1530.

3. Practica denotes the annual astrological prognostication, not a practical handbook.
